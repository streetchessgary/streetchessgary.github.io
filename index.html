    How it works

    1. Book a battle. Checkout asks for your username.
    2. Get in the queue. You'll show up above within a few hours, usually sooner.
    3. Get the challenge. When you're up, I send it on the platform you picked. Be online!

    Will the game be recorded?
    Yes. Games may be streamed or posted as videos, showing your chess username. Booking means you agree to that.

    I'll try once more later the same day. If we still can't connect, email me to reschedule.
    Refunds?
    If I can't play you within 7 days, you get a full refund.
    Is this a bet?
